Percentage Calculator

Calculate percentages, percentage changes and percentage of a number without an account.

Percentage formulas

X% of Y = X × Y ÷ 100. To find what percent X is of Y, use (X ÷ Y) × 100. Percentage change is ((new − old) ÷ old) × 100, when the old value is nonzero.

About the watermark editor

The editor hides a chosen rectangular area with blur or a solid cover; it does not reconstruct the original hidden pixels or use AI inpainting. It can handle a fixed-position mark, including on videos created with tools such as Gemini, when you have rights to edit them. If the mark moves, this fixed area will not track it.

Export processes the video in real time and requires MediaRecorder and canvas recording support. Audio is included when the browser permits capture. Quality, codec, output size and audio support differ by browser; an original low-resolution video cannot be made genuinely Full HD. Large and long videos can fail on phones. Do not use this to misrepresent a video's origin or ownership.

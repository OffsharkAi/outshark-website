Base64 Text Encoder

Encode UTF-8 text to Base64 and decode Base64 text using a free browser tool.

Base64 is not encryption

Base64 is a reversible data representation, not a way to protect secrets. It can represent UTF-8 text but should not be used to hide passwords or private data.

Text is processed locally in your browser, without uploading content to Outshark.

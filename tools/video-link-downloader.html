Video Link Downloader

Save directly downloadable video files where the host allows browser downloads, or find the official download option for your own social videos.

What links work?

Direct HTTPS or HTTP video files (such as MP4, WebM, MOV) can be saved when their servers permit cross-origin access. If download access is blocked, you can open the original link in your browser and use the hosting site's own download option.

Instagram, Facebook and YouTube links

Ordinary post, Reel and watch-page links are not direct video files. This free static website does not bypass login, platform controls, DRM or access restrictions, and does not promise HD copies of social posts. Use the service's own export or download option for videos you own or have permission to save.

Outshark does not proxy, store, or scrape social media videos. Video resolution depends on the actual file, not the download button.
